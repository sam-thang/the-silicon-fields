Two throwaway prototypes draw the same 41 approved projects from data/projects.geojson. Open each on your iPhone and note how it feels and what the meter says.

Open MapLibre Open deck.gl

Each page plays the timeline once on load. Add ?play=0 to skip it.

What to try on each

  1. Watch the opening play from 2020 to today. Is it smooth, and do the colors change at the right moments?
  2. Spin and pinch the globe. Note the fps and worst figures while you move it.
  3. Drag the timeline back and forth quickly.
  4. Tap columns in Texas and the US Midwest, where they sit close together. Does the right project come up?
  5. Tap “Run load test” (10× the columns, to stand in for a weaker phone) and repeat steps 2 and 3.
